SECTION C — SYSTEM ANALYSIS

Failure Mode: Notice Failure

In rule-based systems, actions often depend on the assumption that information has been successfully received and processed by the affected party. When confirmation of receipt is absent or unreliable, the system may still proceed as if notice were complete.

This produces notice failure. The system treats non-response as non-compliance, even when the information pathway itself is compromised.

Because the enforcement logic assumes successful transmission, downstream actions appear procedurally justified while resting on an unverified premise.

Notice failure is not refusal. It is an unconfirmed delivery state.

Systems that do not validate receipt before escalation will repeatedly misclassify absence of response as intent.
