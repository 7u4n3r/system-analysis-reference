SECTION C — SYSTEM ANALYSIS

Failure Mode: Escalation Without Update

Rule-driven systems frequently lack an interrupt mechanism once an enforcement pathway is active. When updated conditions are not designed to halt ongoing processes, escalation continues by default.

This produces escalation without update. Actions intensify even though the original triggering condition no longer exists.

The system is not responding to reality. It is responding to its prior state.

This behavior is often misread as intent when it is actually inertia.
