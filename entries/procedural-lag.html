SECTION C — SYSTEM ANALYSIS

Failure Mode: Procedural Lag

In complex systems, corrective information often enters through a different channel than enforcement actions. When those channels are not synchronized, the system continues to act on obsolete data.

This creates procedural lag. The system technically possesses updated information, but its decision layer has not incorporated it.

As a result, actions remain internally consistent while becoming externally inaccurate.

Procedural lag is not misconduct. It is a timing failure.

Systems without synchronization mechanisms will reproduce this pattern predictably.
